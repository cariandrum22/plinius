# Context

A growing marketplace startup needs full-text product search with filters,
typo-tolerance, and relevance ranking. Engineering is debating whether to build
search in-house (on an open-source search engine they self-host), adopt a managed
search service, or extend their existing relational database with full-text
features. The team is small; search is important to conversion but is not the
company's core differentiator.

# Your task

1. Frame the build-vs-buy decision. What factors should drive it here (team
   size, differentiation, total cost of ownership, time to market, control,
   lock-in)?
2. Evaluate the three options against those factors.
3. Make a recommendation for *this* company's stage and justify it. State the
   conditions under which you would revisit the decision.
4. Name the main risk of your recommendation.

Keep the reasoning grounded in this specific context.
