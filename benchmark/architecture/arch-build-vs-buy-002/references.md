## Intent

Probes structured decision-making under the build-vs-buy trade-off, tied to company stage and strategic differentiation rather than technical novelty.

## Assumptions

Assume a small team and that search is important but not the core differentiator. There is no 'right' vendor; the reasoning is judged.

## Reviewer notes

A strong answer establishes decision criteria first, evaluates each option against them, and ties the recommendation to stage and differentiation with a revisit trigger and a named risk. A weak answer picks a technology on preference, ignores total cost of ownership or lock-in, or fails to connect the choice to this company's situation.
