## Intent

Probes strategic domain modeling: the ability to find semantic seams in a complex domain where the same words carry different meanings, and to express inter-context relationships and translation boundaries.

## Assumptions

Assume a genuinely large domain with conflicting departmental vocabularies. The reviewer should not expect a specific number of contexts, but should expect the polysemy of key terms to be handled deliberately.

## Reviewer notes

A strong answer draws contexts around linguistic and organizational seams, explicitly models the same term differently per context, and uses named relationship patterns with anticorruption where models must not leak. It resists one giant canonical model. A weak answer produces database tables or CRUD services labeled as contexts, ignores the polysemy, or proposes a single shared 'Policy' entity used everywhere.
