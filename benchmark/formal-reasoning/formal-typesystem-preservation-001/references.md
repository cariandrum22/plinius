## Intent

Probes reviewing a type-preservation proof sketch: judging the induction principle, identifying auxiliary lemmas a sketch relies on implicitly, and separating 'the theorem is true' from 'this proof is valid'.

## Assumptions

Assume the standard call-by-value small-step semantics implied by the given rules and standard inversion of the typing relation; the language is the well-known typed-arithmetic core.

## Reviewer notes

A strong answer judges the induction principle correctly, identifies the auxiliary lemmas the sketch silently relies on and where they are used, addresses the congruence cases, and separates 'the theorem is true' from 'this sketch proves it'. A weak answer accepts the sketch wholesale, declares the theorem false, or misidentifies where a missing lemma is needed.
