## Intent

Probes careful reasoning about quantifier order and scope, the relative strength of two quantifier orderings, and vacuous satisfaction -- foundational specification-reading skills.

## Assumptions

Assume classical first-order semantics with the given predicates, a fixed non-empty set of clients, and that 'eventually' is a temporal modality the answer treats consistently. Read the English as the source of truth for intent.

## Reviewer notes

A strong answer renders both formulas precisely, identifies which is strictly stronger and justifies the implication direction with a distinguishing scenario, and flags the vacuous-satisfaction case with a guard. A weak answer reverses the implication direction, treats the two formulas as equivalent, or misses the vacuity issue.
