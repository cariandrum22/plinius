## Intent

Probes reviewing a termination plus partial-correctness sketch: demanding an explicit decreasing measure and an explicit preservation invariant, and separating a true conclusion from a rigorous proof.

## Assumptions

Assume positive integer inputs, standard integer arithmetic, and the known fact gcd(a,b)=gcd(a-b,b) for a>b (whose justification the reviewer may require the proof to cite).

## Reviewer notes

A strong answer supplies an explicit natural-number measure and verifies strict decrease each iteration including the lopsided case, names the preservation invariant and flags that the sketch merely asserts its key arithmetic fact, and concludes the theorem is true while the sketch is not yet rigorous, listing the specific gaps. A weak answer accepts 'the values shrink' as a measure, misses the unjustified arithmetic fact, or declares the algorithm wrong.
